## Decision

The lead ranks the milestone and defines its acceptance. A delivery manager dispatches independent reviews, qualifies exact candidates, routes corrections, and may merge approved offline work when reviewed and tested code matches the landing and hosted CI passes. Escalate a change in acceptance, an unresolved material finding or live authorization to the lead. The lead remains the shared board writer.

Use the recipient's durable queue for routine events. Waiting for an exact result or reaching an explicit handoff enables delivery. Preserve drafts and suspend refill under an owner pause. Pickup requires acknowledgment. For the native swap, prioritize a recoverable closed launch receipt and offline qualification of the selected DeepSeek and Qwen targets. HY4 Flash configuration follows the pair preparation independently.
