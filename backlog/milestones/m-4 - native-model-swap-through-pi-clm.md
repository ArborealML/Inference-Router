## Description

Validate selector parity and exclusive host admission through verified readiness. Complete a pi turn on A and a streamed turn on B after an observed native swap on an authorized host. SPEC §§5.3–5.5 and 7.

The local Pi switch passed on November 21 and shipped in [PR #75](https://github.com/ArborealML/Inference-Router/pull/75). The original queued request drove the physical switch after the incumbent request finished. Docker, authenticated backend readback and GPU-process observations confirmed the new resident. Cross-process enforcement on shared hosts remains open. [Live verification](../../docs/local-model-switching.md#verification)
